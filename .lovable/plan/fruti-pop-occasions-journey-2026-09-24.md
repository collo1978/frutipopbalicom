# Fruti Pop Occasions Journey

## Goal
Create one complete Occasions page that carries visitors from a homepage moment card to the relevant story, then to one shared enquiry form with the occasion already selected.

## Homepage and navigation
- Keep the five existing moment cards and their current photos, titles, and descriptions.
- Point each card to its matching section on `/occasions` and add a centred green **Explore All Occasions →** button below the cards.
- Replace the desktop Occasions dropdown and mobile occasion sublinks with one standard **Occasions** link.
- Keep the green WhatsApp Order button unchanged.

## Main Occasions page
- Replace the overview card grid with four full-width story sections: Birthday Parties, Schools & Sports Clubs, Events, and Villas & Poolside.
- Use the supplied labels, headlines, descriptions, and CTA text exactly.
- Reuse existing real photos, alternating image and text positions on desktop and stacking cleanly on mobile.
- Give each section a stable anchor so homepage links and old URLs land at the right story.
- Place the existing Family Pack presentation after the four stories and before the form, with **Get Your Party Pops →** scrolling to the form without changing its selection.

## Shared enquiry flow
- Keep one enquiry form at the bottom with the requested heading and supporting text.
- Limit its occasion choices to the five requested options on this page.
- Make each story CTA scroll to the visible form heading and preselect the matching occasion.
- Preserve WhatsApp and email behavior so every completed field and the selected occasion are included.
- Update the quantity label to **How many pops would you like?** for this shared form without changing forms on unrelated pages.

## Old occasion links
- Convert the four old occasion URLs into redirects to the matching anchors on `/occasions`.
- Update all internal occasion links to the new single-page journey while preserving the old URLs for visitors and bookmarks.

## Verification
- Test homepage card links, the Explore All Occasions button, navigation, all four CTA selections, the Family Pack CTA, WhatsApp and email message contents, and old URL redirects.
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for crops, sticky-header anchor positioning, keyboard use, overflow, broken images, and page errors.
- Keep the project unpublished and leave unrelated pages and ordering behavior unchanged.
